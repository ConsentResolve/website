Targeting + budget (max-aggressive)

  • Objective: Leads / conversions on the /demo event (Pixel must be live). North star = cost per /demo lead; leading indicator = hook rate (3-sec) + thruplay.
  • Audience: broad + Advantage+ placements (Reels/Feed/Stories); layer home-service / small-business-owner / contractor interests; build lookalikes once /demo conversions accumulate.
  • Budget: ~$40–60/creative/day × 3 days → readable signal fast. Round 1 ≈ $1.5–2k across ~12 creatives.
  • Scale rule: at 48–72h kill anything with weak hook rate or cost/lead >2× median; pour budget into the top 3–4; scale winners 20–50%/day; refresh weekly.
  • TikTok: organic daily + Spark Ads boosting the organic posts that pop.
