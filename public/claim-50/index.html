Before you claim

The questions we get every day.

What's the catch on the 50?

There isn't one, but there are rules. Card on file so the meter knows when to start. Nothing charged until lead 51. Leads only count once the script is live. One per shop. Cancel any time — if you walk after 50, you've paid nothing and you keep the report.

What happens at lead 51?

Flat $7 each, billed as they land. No setup charge, no monthly minimum, no contract. You can set a monthly cap so you're never surprised.

Will you sell my leads to the shop down the road?

No. Every lead is yours alone — never resold, never auctioned, never shared. One consent, one contractor.

Do I get phone numbers?

No, and that's deliberate. You get a name and a consented email, plus what they were shopping for. They come back through your funnel and call you. No cold calls, no cold texts — that's what keeps this clean.

Is this legal?

Nobody is identified until the homeowner accepts your consent banner. Matching is deterministic, never fingerprinting. Every reveal is timestamped and signed, and your policies stay current through our Termageddon partnership. It's built to the GDPR standard, so the U.S. state patchwork is covered by design.

Do I have to drop my Google Ads or LSA?

Keep every channel that's working. This sits on top and catches the homeowners those channels already sent you — the ones who were about to walk out with no name.

What actually happens on the 15 minutes?

We share screens, open your Analytics, and count the visitors that left without a name. Then we install the consent banner and watch your first reveal come through. If you want to stop there, you keep the report and the banner.

How do I cancel?

One click in your dashboard, or call us. No contract, no early-out fee, no retention script.
